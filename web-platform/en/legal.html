Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Your Documents & Scans

All scanned documents and files are processed and retained 100% locally in your device's memory (IndexedDB). Your files are never transmitted to ATTA NATION servers, and we have zero access to your document contents.

User Account Information

We store only your name, email address, and a cryptographically salted password hash (PBKDF2-SHA256). This record exists solely to verify proof of purchase and allow you to re-download the application binary on future devices.

Payment Security

Transactions are processed via licensed payment gateway infrastructure (Midtrans / Stripe). We only receive transaction confirmation receipts and never inspect or store your credit card or bank account credentials.

Optional AI Services

If you choose to use the AI document summarizer, document text is sent to Google Gemini exclusively using your own personal API key. This request occurs strictly when you initiate an AI analysis action.

Data Deletion

You may purge your account record at any time from My Account or within the app's Settings menu. Local files in your browser can be deleted anytime via Settings > Clear all local data.
