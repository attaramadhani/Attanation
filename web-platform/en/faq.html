Apps & Platforms

Can I use it on an iPhone?

Yes. Simply open DocStudio Pro in the Safari browser, sign in with your account, tap the Share button, and select Add to Home Screen. It runs standalone like any iOS app.

Does it require an internet connection?

No. Scanning, image processing, PDF merging, compression, and watermarking all run 100% offline in your phone's memory. Internet is only required for checking app updates or optional AI summaries.

What is the user account for?

Your account is created at checkout with your name and email. It allows you to re-download your app installer if you switch phones and sign in easily on iOS.

How does the AI summarization feature work?

Document summarization connects directly to Google Gemini using your personal API key configured in Settings. Heuristic analysis (dates, ID numbers, amounts) always runs offline without any API key.

Data & Security

Where are my documents stored?

Directly in your phone's private IndexedDB storage. Your files never touch our servers and are never uploaded to the cloud.

How do I transfer documents when getting a new phone?

Open the Backup tab on your old device and select Export All to ZIP. Download the app on your new phone and choose Restore from Backup.

What if I forget my app lock PIN?

You can reset the PIN from the lock screen, but to protect personal privacy, local data is wiped upon reset. We strongly recommend creating regular ZIP backups to Google Drive or iCloud.
